From Artificial Intelligence
to Applied Intelligence

Reframing your relationship with intelligence

Applied Intelligence is a, human-centered approach to Artificial Intelligence (AI). It uses technology to enhance human cognitive capabilities, decision-making, and productivity rather than replacing them. Humans provide context, judgment, and final authority, where AI acts as a collaborative partnership handling data analysis and pattern recognition (left brain serving the right brain).

Key Aspects of
Applied Intelligence

Human Centric Approach

Humans remain central and in control, with the AI providing support, insights, and recommendations, fueling ingenuity.

Purpose

The goal is to improve problem definition, decision‑making and performance, building synergetic systems, creating new horizons.

Applied

Used in high‑stakes settings where AI and human judgment work together, from medical diagnostics and financial decisions to trusted, personalized customer experiences.
