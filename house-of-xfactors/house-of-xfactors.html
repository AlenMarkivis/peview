House of Xfactors

New Horizons

Xoriant’s global home for Human Ingenuity

It’s where we develop the people skills that turn expertise and AI into real impact for clients.

In a world powered by AI, technology alone is no longer enough. The House of X‑Factors (HOXF) is where we grow the human side of tech: judgment, creativity, empathy and collaboration on top of our engineering and AI skills. It brings every country, every acquired company and every practice into one shared space, so we stay ahead in a time of continuous change.

Left brain & right brain,
working together

HOXF is built on balance. On one side, we sharpen left‑brain capabilities – architecture, testing, platforms, security, data and AI tools, so our people stay technically exceptional. On the other, we deliberately develop right‑brain strengths: listening, storytelling, facilitation, creative problem‑defining and design thinking, the skills machines can’t copy.

How it develops your future partners

Each House is a safe space away from project pressure where people can step back, learn and experiment. Engineers learn to tell better business stories, consultants pick up new technical patterns, leaders from new acquisitions bring fresh colors and ways of working; ideas and people constantly “pollinate” across Houses.
